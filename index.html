About RKM Production

Ratanang Kadi Media Production (RKM Production) is a South African film production company founded by Ratanang Kadi, in 2024. RKM Production is dedicated to telling authentic, emotional and impactful stories. From short films to cinematic dramas, we create films that inspire, challenge and connect with audiences.

FOUNDER, FILMMAKER & ACTOR

Ratanang Kadi

Ratanang Kadi is a South African independent filmmaker, screenwriter, and director dedicated to telling authentic African stories. Through RKM Production, he creates emotionally driven films that explore family, culture, youth challenges, identity, and the human spirit.

His work focuses on powerful storytelling that resonates with local communities while speaking to universal human experiences. From rural dramas to socially conscious narratives, his vision is to inspire, educate, and entertain through cinema.
